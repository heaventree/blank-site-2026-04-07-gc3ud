Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about the JAM Safety OS platform.

What is the uptime guarantee for the platform? expand_more
JAM Safety OS is hosted on enterprise-grade cloud infrastructure with 99.9% uptime SLA. We monitor the platform 24/7 and maintain redundant systems across multiple availability zones. During the hypercare period (first 4 weeks post-launch), our team provides daily standups to address any issues immediately.
How do I reset my password? expand_more
Click "Forgot Password" on the login screen. Enter your email address and you'll receive a password reset link within 2 minutes. If you don't see it, check your spam folder. For security reasons, the reset link expires after 24 hours. Contact support if you need additional assistance.
What browsers are supported? expand_more
JAM Safety OS works on all modern browsers: Chrome 90+, Firefox 88+, Safari 14+, Edge 90+. The Staff Field Portal is a progressive web app (PWA) and works on iOS and Android devices. For the best experience, keep your browser updated to the latest version.
How is my data backed up? expand_more
All data is automatically backed up every 6 hours to geographically redundant storage. We retain daily backups for 30 days and weekly backups for 1 year. In the unlikely event of data loss, we can restore to any point within the retention window. Backups are encrypted and tested regularly.
Can I export my data? expand_more
Yes. All portals have one-click export functionality for reports, incident data, manpower records and safety documents. Exports are available in PDF, CSV and Excel formats. You can also request a full data export via GDPR data portability requests — we'll deliver a complete dataset within 30 days.
How do I add new users to the platform? expand_more
Ops Admins can add users directly in the JAM Ops Portal under Settings → User Management. You can assign roles (Ops Admin, Ops Staff, Client Senior, Client Junior, Subcontractor, Field Staff) and send automated invite emails. Bulk user imports via CSV are also available for seasonal staff onboarding.
What happens if I forget my MFA code? expand_more
If you've lost access to your authenticator app, contact support immediately with proof of identity. We can disable MFA temporarily and send you a recovery code via email. For security, we'll ask you to re-enable MFA on your next login.
Is the Staff Field Portal available offline? expand_more
Yes. The Staff Field Portal is a progressive web app that works completely offline. Forms are cached locally and synced automatically when connectivity returns. This is especially useful at venues with poor signal. No internet connection is required to submit reports, inspections or incident forms.
How do I report a security vulnerability? expand_more
Please report security issues to security@heaventree.io rather than using the public support channels. Include a detailed description of the vulnerability and steps to reproduce it. We'll acknowledge receipt within 24 hours and work with you on a fix. Do not share vulnerability details publicly until we've issued a patch.

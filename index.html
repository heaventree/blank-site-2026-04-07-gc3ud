NexOS Platform Architecture

What Heaventree builds for JAM Safety is the configuration, the portals, the workflows and the safety-industry intelligence — on top of a proven, battle-tested foundation. Component dictionary — what each piece does
lock
Auth & RBAC
Authentication and role-based access control

Handles login, session management (JWT + refresh tokens) and enforces six distinct roles: Ops Admin, Ops Staff, Client Senior, Client Junior, Subcontractor and Field Staff. Every API endpoint checks the caller's role before responding. MFA enforced for Ops Admin. This is the first thing built in Week 1 — nothing else is secure without it.

folder_open
Document Engine
Versioned document store with template rendering

Stores and versions all 13 safety document types. Documents uploaded by JAM Ops are instantly accessible in the Client Portal and Staff Field Portal — same document, no sync lag. Supports template-based generation (the AI agents produce documents here). Tracks last-updated, expiry dates and read receipts per user.

account_tree
Workflow Engine
Approval routing, escalation and notifications

Powers every multi-step process: subcontractor document approvals, PTW issuance, incident escalation, and invoice sign-off. Configurable RACI routing — when Conor tells us who approves what, we programme it here. Tracks state (pending → approved/rejected), records who acted and when, and sends automatic chaser emails when deadlines approach.

smart_toy
Agent Orchestrator
NexOS AI Runtime — CrewDock agent orchestration

The brain of the AI layer. When an ops user triggers "generate ESMP" or "create CV", the orchestrator queues the task, calls the appropriate Claude-powered agent, tracks the job status, and delivers the result back to the UI. Handles retries, timeouts and cost tracking. Runs on NexOS's built-in CrewDock agent runtime, configured with JAM Safety-specific agent definitions.

extension
MCP Tool Registry
Model Context Protocol — tools available to AI agents

Lists the tools AI agents are allowed to call: read a staff profile, look up event details, fetch a document template, query weather data. MCP (Anthropic's open standard) ensures agents can only access data they're authorised to use. New tools are registered here as the platform grows — no code changes to the agents needed.

sync_alt
Field Sync Engine
Offline-first architecture for the Staff Field PWA

Field staff at venues often have poor connectivity. The sync engine uses a Service Worker + IndexedDB in the browser to cache forms locally. Staff can submit reports with zero signal — data queues on device and syncs automatically when connectivity returns. Conflict resolution handles the edge case where the same record is edited offline by two people.

cloud_sync
MS Graph Bridge
Bidirectional Microsoft 365 integration

Connects to JAM's Microsoft 365 tenant via the Graph API. SharePoint sync: documents created or approved in NexOS are automatically pushed to the relevant SharePoint library. Outlook monitoring: the finance module watches a designated inbox for PO receipts and client emails, extracting key data to pre-populate invoices — eliminating double-entry. Requires Graph API access from client IT (needed by Week 10).

payments
Finance Module
Purchase orders, quotes, invoices and revenue tracking

Generates POs, quotes and invoices from project data — no more manual spreadsheet work. Revenue auto-populates from MS Graph Outlook monitoring (when a PO arrives by email, the system reads it). Revenue Management in the Client Portal gives senior clients a live view of their spend. Tracks profitability per event for the analytics dashboard. Not a payment processor — it manages the paper trail, not the money flow.

How the portals talk to each other
JAM Ops ↔ Client Portal
Documents approved in Ops are instantly visible in the Client Portal. Incident reports filed by field staff appear in both. Revenue data flows from Ops Finance into Client Revenue Management.
JAM Ops ↔ Subcontractor Portal
Ops reviews and approves/rejects subcontractor submissions. PTW documents issued in Ops appear in the Subcontractor Portal immediately. Insurance expiry alerts trigger in Ops from subcontractor submission data.
Staff Field ↔ JAM Ops
Every form submitted by field staff (site report, inspection, incident, toolbox talk) flows into the JAM Ops dashboard in real time. Time sheets from Field flow into Ops for approval. Daily site reports consolidate into Ops compliance view.
Document Engine ↔ All Portals
The document library is shared. JAM Ops uploads the Event Safety Management Plan; the Client Portal and Staff Field Portal read the same version-controlled document instantly — there is no separate copy or manual distribution.

Portal deep-dive

Every feature from your whiteboards, explained. What it does, why it matters, and how it connects to the rest of the platform. Colour-coded to delivery tier: MVP by Week 9   MVP+ Weeks 10–16   MVP++ Future SOW.

hub
JAM Ops Portal
Internal command centre — JAM Safety operations team only
Dashboard & Project Management MVP
Central command view — all active events, staff assignments, outstanding approvals and compliance alerts at a glance. Project CRUD with double-booking detection prevents the same staff member being assigned to two concurrent events.
Incident Management MVP
Log, assign, escalate and resolve on-site incidents with full audit trail. Incidents submitted via the Staff Field Portal appear here instantly. Structured resolution workflow with time-to-close tracking and regulatory compliance trail.
Daily Site Reports — Compliance MVP
Aggregated view of all daily site reports submitted by field staff. Filter by event, date, site and compliance status. Flags missing reports. Exportable for client reporting and regulatory submissions.
Documentation / Deliverables Tracker MVP
Kanban-style view of all documents owed to each client — submitted, outstanding, overdue. Automatic chase reminders. Directly connected to the Document Engine — approving a doc here makes it live in the Client Portal instantly.
Inspection Scheduler MVP
Schedule recurring site inspections with assigned inspector, recurrence rules (daily/weekly/per-event) and photo evidence slots. Field staff complete inspections via the Field Portal — results sync back here automatically.
Client Documentation Repository MVP
Upload and version-control all client-facing safety documents. Changes are live in the Client Portal within seconds — no emailing, no version confusion. Tracks who viewed what and when.
ESMP Generator (AI) MVP+
Fill in a structured brief (event type, venue, expected crowd, risk categories) and Claude generates a complete, JAM-standard Event Safety Management Plan. Ops reviews, edits and approves — the AI does the first draft. Delivered in under 2 minutes.
JAM Format CV Creator (AI) MVP+
Select a staff member and Claude generates a polished, consistently formatted CV in JAM's branded style — pulling experience, qualifications and certifications from their profile. Eliminates manual CV reformatting entirely.
Q-Schedule Generator (AI) MVP+
Input event parameters (type, scale, duration, venue) and Claude produces a quotation schedule — staffing numbers by role, day/night splits, supervisor ratios. Dramatically speeds up quote preparation. Ops refines and approves before sending.
SharePoint / Outlook Intelligence MVP+
Bidirectional MS Graph integration. Approved documents push to SharePoint automatically. A designated Outlook inbox is monitored for PO receipts and client emails — key data extracts to pre-populate invoices. Requires MS365 Graph API access from client IT.
Finance Module MVP+
Generate POs, quotes and invoices from project data. Revenue auto-populates from Outlook PO monitoring. No more double-entry between email and spreadsheet. Profitability tracked per event feeds directly into the analytics dashboard.
Analytics Dashboard MVP+
Utilisation rates by role and event, profitability per project, compliance rates by site. Emergency & overtime workflow tracking. The operational intelligence layer that turns data into decisions.
Time Sheet Management + TOIL MVP+
Hours submitted by field staff flow here for review and approval before payroll export. TOIL (time off in lieu) accrual and approval managed in the same module. Big Projects capacity planner shows staff allocation across concurrent large events.
